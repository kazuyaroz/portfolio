Chia Network — clvm_rs Algorithmic Complexity DoS
High · CVSS 7.5 Submitted #410883
Identified an algorithmic complexity vulnerability in the Chia Rust-native CLVM bridge where bytes(program) or repr(program) materializes an expanded serialization of 2^(N+1)-1 bytes, leading to OOM conditions (N=20→2MB, N=30→2GB, N=40→2TB). No authentication required. PoC with measured timing (4,096ms vs 0.02ms baseline), root cause analysis, and negative control.
Arc (Circle) — Sign RPC Attestation Bypass
Medium · CVSS 6.0 Duplicate #4159903
The Sign RPC in arc-remote-signer does not validate enclave attestation freshness, while PublicKey does. Broken security invariant: a compromised enclave with expired attestation can continue signing arbitrary messages undetected. Independently discovered; closed as duplicate of prior submission, confirming validity.
